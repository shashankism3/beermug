About Us

AmritBharatExpress.com is an independent Indian Railways passenger information portal dedicated exclusively to India's modern push-pull non-AC superfast train network—the Amrit Bharat Express.

Our Editorial Mission

Introduced to provide high-speed, jerk-free, and affordable long-distance rail travel across major corridors of India, Amrit Bharat Express trains feature upgraded LHB coaches, semi-permanent couplers, ergonomic seating, individual mobile charging sockets, and enhanced safety systems. Our goal is to organize verified timetables, intermediate station halts, coach sequence layouts, route maps, and practical boarding guidance into a clean, fast-loading reference for everyday travelers.

Data Accuracy & Non-Affiliation

All schedules, coach formations, and station distances published on this website are compiled from public railway notifications and National Train Enquiry System (NTES) timetables. While we regularly audit and update route pages whenever new trains are flagged off or timings are revised, AmritBharatExpress.com is an independent informational resource and is not affiliated with Indian Railways, the Ministry of Railways, or IRCTC.

Disclaimer: AmritBharatExpress.com is an independent informational travel portal and is not affiliated, endorsed by, or connected with Indian Railways or IRCTC. Railway schedules, fares, and coach compositions are subject to operational revisions; passengers should verify details via official IRCTC/NTES channels before travel.
