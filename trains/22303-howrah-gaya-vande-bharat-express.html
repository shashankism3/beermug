22303 | Howrah - Gaya Vande Bharat Express

The 22303 Howrah - Gaya Vande Bharat Express is a semi-high-speed day-intercity air-conditioned chair car trainset operated by Indian Railways between Howrah and Gaya. Featuring indigenous Train 18 distributed traction technology, automatic sliding doors, and GPS-based passenger infotainment displays, it connects major commercial and cultural hubs along the corridor.

  • Train Category: Vande Bharat Express
  • Inauguration/Launch Date: 15 September 2024
  • Origin: Howrah
  • Destination: Gaya
  • Travel Classes: AC Chair Car (CC) & Executive Chair Car (EC)
  • Key Corridor Stations: Howrah, Durgapur, Asansol, Dhanbad, Parasnath, Koderma, Gaya

Passenger Travel, Catering & Booking Guide

Trainset Technology & On-Board Comfort

Built with aerodynamic nose cones and underslung propulsion equipment, the 22303 Howrah - Gaya Vande Bharat Express offers rapid acceleration, aircraft-style diffused LED lighting, 180-degree rotating seats in Executive Chair Car (EC), reclining ergonomic seats in AC Chair Car (CC), touch-free bio-vacuum lavatories, and onboard Wi-Fi infotainment.

Onboard Catering & Refreshments

Passengers traveling on Train 22303 can opt for onboard IRCTC catering—including morning tea, breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, or dinner depending on train timings—or purchase refreshments from onboard pantry staff.

Coach Composition, Booking Rules & Live Running Status

A standard 16-coach Vande Bharat Express rake features 14 AC Chair Car coaches (C1–C14) and 2 Executive Chair Car coaches (E1–E2) (8-coach mini rakes operate with C1–C7 and E1 on select routes). Tickets can be booked up to 60 days in advance on IRCTC, with Tatkal booking opening at 10:00 AM IST the day before travel.

Check live running status via Indian Railways NTES or book reserved seats on the official IRCTC Next Generation eTicketing Portal.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What seating classes are available on 22303 Howrah - Gaya Vande Bharat Express?

Train 22303 operates with two air-conditioned seating classes: AC Chair Car (CC) in a 3x2 seating layout and Executive Chair Car (EC) in a 2x2 rotating-seat layout.

Is food compulsory when booking Vande Bharat Express tickets?

No. Onboard catering is optional on IRCTC; passengers can choose 'No Food' during booking if they do not wish to include meal charges in their fare.

Can I board 22303 Howrah - Gaya Vande Bharat Express with an unreserved (UTS) general ticket?

No. Vande Bharat trains are 100% reserved air-conditioned trainsets. Passengers must hold a valid confirmed reservation before boarding.
