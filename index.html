About Indian Railways Amrit Bharat Express

The Amrit Bharat Express is Indian Railways' flagship non-air-conditioned LHB superfast train series engineered for affordable, long-distance intercity travel. Powered by dual WAP-5 locomotives at both ends in a push-pull configuration, these trains achieve faster acceleration and deceleration at intermediate stations while eliminating jerks through semi-permanent couplers.

Each rake provides cushioned 3-tier Sleeper Class berths and ergonomic General Seating (GS/GN) coaches fitted with individual mobile charging sockets, bottle holders, foldable snack tables, zero-discharge bio-toilets, and CCTV cameras.
